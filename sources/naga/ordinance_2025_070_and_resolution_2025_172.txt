SECTION 6. ENFORCEMENT AND IMPLEMENTATION. The Naga City Police
Office, its Traffic Enforcement Unit (TEU), and the Public Safety
Office (PSO) are authorized to oversee and coordinate the
implementation of the LPTRP. Only franchises or CPCs consistent with
these routes and unit allocations shall be endorsed for approval to
the LTFRB. The routing plan shall be subject to periodic evaluation
and amendment in accordance with LTFRB/DOTr and Sangguniang
Panlungsod procedures. Existing ordinances relating to transport and
traffic management which carries penalties for violations consistent
with this ordinance shall apply.
